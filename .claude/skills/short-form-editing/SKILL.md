# Short-form editing (Premiere / After Effects)

This is the **editing inside the video**. It has nothing to do with the `before-after` skill, which only builds the side-by-side showcase layout.

The full breakdown with exact timings, settings, the caption system, B-roll building, transitions, look and SFX is in [TRICKS.md](TRICKS.md).

## Core rules
- Every sentence gets a small **setup line** that appears in sync with the speech (blur → sharp per word), plus a big **punch word** in yellow `#FFE21A` with a Glow. Its letters pop in random order, with an SFX.
- Something visual changes every 3–4 seconds: a jump cut or punch-in, a B-roll build, an element behind the subject, or a flash.
- The B-roll is a designed top-down desk scene built one element at a time, not stock footage.
- Look: a black gradient at the bottom, a teal and orange grade, a light halftone texture, and a slow push-in.
- **Caption font:** Alexandria, unless the user says otherwise.
