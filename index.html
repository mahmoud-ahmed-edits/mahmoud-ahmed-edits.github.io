About Me

I'm Mahmoud, a professional video editor with 4+ years of experience. I edit both long-form YouTube videos and short-form content — Shorts, Reels and TikToks — along with cinematic pieces and commercial ads.

Short-form is where I really shine: strong hooks in the first second, fast pacing and clean captions. In long-form, I focus on storytelling and pacing that keep viewers watching until the end. With polished color grading and sound design, every video feels on-brand — whether you're a creator growing your channel or a brand launching a campaign.
